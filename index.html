A quick guide to this little computer

Open something
Double-click an icon on the desktop or in a folder. On a touchscreen, tap. With a keyboard, press Enter.
Explore the folders
My Computer → My Little World (C:) → Research, Essays, or Photography. Use Back or Up to retrace your steps.
Browse photographs
Open Photography to browse thumbnails. Open a picture to view it, then use Previous and Next, or the left and right arrow keys. Switch between Fit to Window and Actual Size.
Arrange your windows
Drag a title bar to move a window, or drag an edge to resize it. Double-click a title bar to maximize; double-click again to restore.
Switch and minimize
Click a window to bring it to the front. Use its taskbar button to switch, minimize, or restore it. Show Desktop tucks all your windows away.
Use your keyboard
Use the arrow keys to select icons. Open a window menu with the icon at its top-left corner, then choose Move or Size. Adjust with the arrow keys; Enter confirms and Esc cancels.
Read and explore
Browse the writing, photographs, and music. This desktop is a personal collection to explore.
